Multiplicities cannot express this; it is carried by the `startTime` and `endTime` attributes and checked in the activity diagram.- **R3:** a blocked room cannot accept a new booking. Carried by `Room.isBlocked` and checked in the activity diagram.### 4.3 Assumptions- A1: A booking that ends exactly when another begins (touching at end/start time) does not overlap with it. This is the same decision the AI made in its use-case note, and I keep it.- A2: Blocking a room does not cancel bookings that already exist. R3 only says a blocked room cannot accept a *new* booking, so existing active bookings stay `ACTIVE`; the Administrator has to handle them outside this model.- A3: A cancelled booking is not deleted; its status changes to `CANCELLED`. Only `ACTIVE` bookings count for R2.- A4: A booking has no fixed length: the student chooses the start and end, and the duration must satisfy R1 (greater than 0, at most 2 hours).### 4.4 Findings| # | Element | Problem | Rule or story | Fix || --- | --- | --- | --- | --- || 1 | Booking (note) | The AI's original had no note for the no-overlap rule. Conclusion (120–180 words)The AI got the use-case diagram most wrong, because its own assumptions contradicted the requirements. It declared "a booking lasts exactly 2 hours", but R1 allows any duration above 0 and at most 2 hours; coded literally, a one-hour booking would be rejected. It also called all six use cases independent goals with no include, although R4 says a successful booking produces a confirmation, and it linked Administrator to the confirmation, which US-06 gives to Student. The error most likely to reach the code is the overlap check: the activity step "Check overlapping bookings" never said same room, so a booking could be rejected because of another room's booking. I missed that; the critique caught it, and it also flagged the unsupported "Enter booking details" step, the name and email attributes, and my own first R2 note, which described the booking association instead of non-overlap. The critique's claim that an Administrator class is missing was false: actors need not be domain classes, since no story requires storing administrators.
